Leaks, floods and soaked floors in Marlborough homes

Water damage in Marlborough rarely announces itself. A small drip under a sink, a dishwasher hose that wept for a month or a bathroom ceiling that started to sag after a storm are the kind of jobs that fill the schedule. State labor market data lists Marlborough as part of the Keene area, with NH 12, NH 101 and NH 124 all serving the town.

The same data shows Marlborough Water Works supplies water to the town. That means many homes run on a pressurized public supply, so a split line can put a lot of water on a floor in a short time. Knowing where your main shutoff is before an emergency is worth five minutes.

Does a public water line make a leak worse?

It can, because pressure keeps water coming until you close the valve. Find your main shutoff before you need it.

Can you dry a wall without cutting it open?

Sometimes. Light moisture in clean drywall may dry in place with air movers. Soft or crumbly drywall usually has to be cut out.
