Island, Pearl and West Streets

The City of Keene places the heart of its historic Italian neighborhood around Island, Pearl and West Streets. New Hampshire Public Radio describes it as a residential area west of downtown that sprang up in the early 20th century, as Italian immigrants arrived for railroad and factory jobs. The William Marconi Italian Club is a few blocks from Island Street.

Since then Keene State College has grown nearby and strip malls have appeared along the edges, but many families still live in the neighborhood. No official boundary was found, so we describe it by those three streets.

What we know and what we do not

We found no city record describing the age, size or building type of the homes here, so we do not make claims about them. A house is a house until a crew looks at it. What we can say is that water damage there follows the same rules as anywhere else, and the same quick steps limit the harm.

Pearl Street and the Ashuelot

Keene's 2025 hazard mitigation plan lists a parcel on Pearl Street among the sites considered for acquisition to create floodplain storage. That tells us the city sees the area as relevant to river management. It does not say any home has flooded. No flood-zone finding is verified for these streets, so ask the city or FEMA about your specific address.

Insurance and old houses

An insurer decides coverage from the cause of the loss and the terms of your policy, not from the age of the house. Ask early whether a sudden pipe failure, a roof leak or a drain backup is covered, and keep photos and receipts. Our moisture logs describe what was wet and when.

Italian Neighborhood water damage questions

Which streets does this page cover?

The heart of the neighborhood is Island, Pearl and West Streets, as the City of Keene describes them. We also take calls from nearby streets on the same side of downtown.

Do you work on older homes?

Yes. We read moisture in each material and adjust drying to what the building is made of, including plaster, wood framing and older floors.

Is Pearl Street a flood area?

The city's hazard plan lists a Pearl Street parcel for floodplain storage, but it does not say homes flood there. We cannot confirm a flood zone for your address, so check FEMA's map.

Can I clean up the water myself?

Small clean spills are fine. For anything that soaked walls, floors or insulation, or that came from a drain, call a professional. Hidden moisture can stay behind surfaces that feel dry.
