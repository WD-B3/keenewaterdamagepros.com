Winter, thaw and spring: Westmoreland's water seasons

Westmoreland was settled in 1741 by people who canoed up the Connecticut River, according to the town, and it stayed an agricultural community with mills and small industries through the 1800s. Many of its houses and barns are old. Old farmhouses often have uninsulated crawl spaces, long pipe runs to outbuildings and attic roofs that grow ice dams after a snowy week.

That pattern gives the year a rhythm. Cold snaps split pipes in unheated rooms. A thaw sends meltwater toward foundations. April rain finds the one flashing that has failed. Each season has its own first call, and we use the same rule for all of them: stop the water, then call us, then let us dry the structure before anything is repaired.

When water is spreading in Westmoreland

State municipal data says Westmoreland residents rely on private wells and septic systems, with volunteer fire and EMS and no town police department. For a homeowner that means a well pump and a long driveway are part of any emergency. Call the 24/7 line first, and tell us if the road is icy or the driveway is steep.

Dispatch for Westmoreland is available around the clock, nights and weekends included. The town sits in the Keene labor market and about eleven miles from Cheshire Medical Center, so the same crews that work in Keene serve it. Where we work in Westmoreland

Westmoreland uses ZIP 03467. State records give the town 35.9 square miles of land and about 1,682 residents in the 2017 estimate, with Maplewood Nursing Home among its largest employers. The Park Hill Meeting House and the Corner School are both kept up by the local historical society. The town's 1762 meetinghouse bell was cast by the Paul Revere Foundry. We serve every road in town, from the NH 12 corridor to the hill roads.

Flood and insurance

Frozen pipes, roof leaks and groundwater each fall under different policy language. Sudden events are often handled differently from slow ones, so put your questions to your insurer. Coverage is not something we can promise.

My pipes froze and burst. What should I do first?

Close the main valve, open a tap below the break to drain the line and call us. Do not use a torch on frozen pipe.

Is a wet crawl space a real problem?

It can be. Standing water or constant dampness under a farmhouse feeds rot and mold. See our crawl space page.
