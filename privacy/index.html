Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you call, email or visit this site, and how we use it.

☎ (603) 550-7813

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address and details about the water damage you describe, so that we can connect you with a restoration professional and follow up on the job.

Email

If you email contact@keenewaterdamagepros.com, we keep the message and your address so we can reply. Please do not send sensitive financial details by email.

This website

This site has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. Like most sites, our hosting provider may keep basic server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visited, for security and performance.

How we use information

  • To respond to your call or message.
  • To dispatch a restoration professional to your property.
  • To improve the quality of our service.
  • To meet legal or insurance obligations.

Sharing

We share your contact details and job information with the restoration professional assigned to your property so they can do the work. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing contact@keenewaterdamagepros.com. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page is the current one.
