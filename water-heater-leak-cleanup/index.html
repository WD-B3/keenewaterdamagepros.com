Water heater leak emergency: what to shut off

Start with the heater itself. Turn off the electric breaker, or set a gas unit to pilot or off. Close the cold water supply valve at the top of the tank. If the unit is leaking from the tank body rather than a fitting, it is usually at the end of its life and needs replacing. A plumber replaces it, and we handle the water.

Hot water can scald, so stay clear of the water pooled near the tank. If the heater sits in a basement or utility closet, take care with electrical panels nearby and call the utility if you see water around them. Our 24/7 line is open now if the leak is active.

How a tank failure floods a house

Most tanks fail slowly. A small leak at the base grows over weeks, and the floor around the heater stays wet without anyone noticing. Then a seam splits and the entire tank empties in minutes. Either way, the water collects at the lowest point near the heater, and in many Keene homes that is a basement floor, a utility closet or a first floor laundry room.

In a finished basement, water reaches carpet, drywall and stored boxes quickly. In an upstairs closet it can drip through a ceiling below. If you have lost the water heater and it ran onto a finished floor, see our pages on carpet and basement water damage.

What cleanup after a water heater leak looks like

We extract the water, check how far it traveled with a moisture meter and remove wet materials that will not dry, such as carpet pad or baseboard. Then we set air movers and a dehumidifier. A tank leak is usually clean supply water, which makes cleanup simpler than a flood, but if the water sat for days it can still grow mold.

Mineral scale and rust staining are common around old tanks. Those marks on a concrete floor are cosmetic once the area is dry, and we will tell you which stains are just discoloration. We also record the readings so you can show your insurer when the area dried.

Replace or repair: what to ask the plumber

QuestionWhy it matters
Is the tank body leaking?A body leak cannot be repaired. The unit needs replacement.
Is it a fitting, valve or relief line?These are often repairable without replacing the tank.
Is a drain pan or leak sensor present?Pans and sensors reduce damage from the next failure.
How old is the unit?Read the date on the label. Age is the first thing a plumber will ask about.

What affects the cost of water heater leak cleanup

Price follows scope, and we explain it before work begins.

  • Volume of water. A slow weep costs less than a full tank release
  • Time it sat. Standing water for days spreads into more materials
  • Finished materials. Carpet, drywall and stored goods add removal and drying
  • Drying days. Utility closets are small, but wet drywall and carpet nearby can still add days
  • Access. Tight utility closets and stairs add labor

Insurance and water heater leak cleanup

Many policies pay for the water damage from a sudden tank failure but not for the heater itself, and long-term seepage is treated differently. Check your policy or ask your insurer. Approval is theirs to give, and our job is to leave you with clear records.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup questions, answered

My tank is leaking after dark. Can you come?

The line is open around the clock. Shut off power or gas and the cold supply, then call. We respond the same day when we can, and we do not name an arrival time before we know the job.

Do you replace water heaters?

No. A plumber replaces the heater. We remove the water, dry the structure and document the loss. We work alongside the plumber when timing matters.

The leak seems small. Do I still need drying?

Possibly. A small leak that ran for weeks can soak a floor and the bottom of drywall. A moisture meter tells us whether the area is dry or still holding water.

Can leaked water heater water grow mold?

Yes, if the area stays damp for more than a day or two. Drying quickly lowers that risk. If you already see mold, read our mold remediation page.
