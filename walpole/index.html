Walpole, North Walpole and Drewsville: three places, three ZIPs

State records give Walpole 35.7 square miles of land and another square mile of water, with about 3,710 residents in the 2013 estimate. The town is reached on NH 12 and NH 123, and I-91 in Vermont is about four to five miles away. The ZIPs follow the villages: 03608 for Walpole, 03609 for North Walpole and 03604 for Drewsville.

The same profile lists 1,626 housing units, of which 1,242 are single-family homes and 236 sit in two-to-four-unit buildings. Multi-unit buildings add a wrinkle, because one tenant's leak can wet a neighbor's ceiling. That is why we ask who lives above and below when you call.

When water is spreading in Walpole

Walpole is the farthest of our five towns, about twelve miles northwest of Keene, so the sooner you call, the sooner we can plan the run. The 24/7 line is staffed all night. Emergency water removal is available in every village. No arrival time is promised, and you will hear honestly how long the drive and setup will take.

The state profile lists Walpole Water as the supplier, with private septic systems and municipal service in different parts of town. If your home is on septic and a drain backs up, treat the water as contaminated. Do not wade in it, and keep children and pets out until we have cleaned and sanitized the area.
