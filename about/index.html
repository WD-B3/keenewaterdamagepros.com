About Keene Water Damage Pros

We answer water damage calls in Keene, NH and nearby Cheshire County towns, day or night. This page explains how we work and what we will and will not promise.

☎ (603) 550-7813

What we do

We connect Keene-area homeowners and businesses with licensed and insured restoration professionals for water extraction, structural drying, mold remediation and the work around them. The line is staffed 24 hours a day, because water damage does not follow business hours.

Our service list runs from burst pipes and floods to ceiling leaks and mold. See the full list on our services page.

How we work

  • Scope and price are laid out up front.
  • We use moisture meters and a thermal camera to find where the water went.
  • We dry until readings match unaffected materials, not until it looks dry.
  • We give you photos and daily readings you can share with your insurer.
  • We tell you honestly when a job is outside what we do, such as waterproofing or rebuilding.

Standards we point to

Two published standards guide this trade: ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage and S520 for mold. Ask any company you call whether its crews work to them, ours included. We use generic wording about licensing on purpose: our dispatch works with licensed and insured professionals, and we do not list individual credentials on this site.

What we will not promise

  • We will not promise that your insurer will approve a claim.
  • We will not promise a specific arrival time.
  • We will not promise that mold will never return after the moisture source is fixed.
  • We will not quote a price before we know the scope.

Why Keene

Keene sits on the Ashuelot River, with older homes near Main Street and Court Street, mill buildings along Railroad Street and a college district. The city has seen river flooding in recent memory, and winter cold splits pipes every year. This site is built around those realities and the towns around Keene: Walpole, Westmoreland, Swanzey, Marlborough and Sullivan.
