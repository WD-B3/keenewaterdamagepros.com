Water Damage Services in Keene, NH

Water gets into a house in a handful of ways. Find the cause that matches what you see, and the page for it explains what we do, what affects the cost and what to do while you wait.

☎ (603) 550-7813

Pipes and plumbing

Cold snaps and aging lines cause most winter calls. Start with burst pipe water damage for a split line. Use frozen pipe water damage for a pipe that has frozen or is about to thaw. A failed tank is covered under water heater leak cleanup, and water rising through a floor under slab leak water damage.

Appliances and HVAC

Hoses, drain lines and pans fail slowly and then all at once. Read about appliance leak water damage for washers, dishwashers and refrigerators, AC leak water damage for clogged condensate lines and kitchen water damage restoration for wet cabinets and floors.

Weather and the building envelope

Storms and snowmelt find weak spots. Start with flood damage cleanup after high water, storm water damage cleanup after wind and rain, basement water damage cleanup for the lowest room, ceiling water damage repair for roof and ice dam leaks and crawl space water damage for wet crawl spaces.

Water removal and drying

Every job needs the water out and the structure dry. Water extraction covers pumping and vacuuming, structural drying and dehumidification covers air movers and dehumidifiers, and sewage backup cleanup covers contaminated water that needs protective handling.

Floors and walls

Once the water is out, the surfaces tell you what has to be saved. See hardwood floor water damage repair, carpet water damage restoration and wall and drywall water damage repair.

Commercial properties and mold

Businesses and multi-unit buildings have their own needs, covered on our commercial water damage restoration page. When moisture has been present long enough to grow mold, see mold remediation.

Not sure which one fits?

Call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. We will point you to the right next step.
