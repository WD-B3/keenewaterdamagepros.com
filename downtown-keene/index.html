Main Street, Central Square and the blocks around them

A 2026 city draft transportation assessment describes the commercial core of downtown along Main Street. The City's walking tour of downtown also covers Church Street and the Beaver Brook corridor nearby. Downtown is not only shops, because the city's 2023 housing study includes a separate Downtown housing vulnerability map.

That mix is what shapes the work. A ground floor business, an office above it and an apartment at the top can all share one set of pipes, one roof and one owner. We found no official downtown boundary, so we describe it by Main Street and Central Square.

When one leak reaches three tenants

In a multi-story building, water follows gravity and framing. A failed supply line on the third floor can wet the ceiling of the second, the walls of the first and the stock in a storefront below. The first job is finding every wet surface, not just the one where the water first showed.

That is also why we ask for the building owner or manager early. Shutoffs, access to other units and decisions about who pays are not ours to make, so the sooner the right people are on the phone the sooner drying can begin.

Beaver Brook at the edge of downtown

The city's draft assessment places streets such as Emerald, Dunbar and Baker near Beaver Brook, and the 2025 hazard mitigation plan notes that the brook's floodplain carries heavy development. We have no evidence that a given downtown building floods. If yours sits near the brook, FEMA's map service can show the mapped zone for an address, and your insurer can explain what a policy covers.

Tenants, owners and insurance

Who is responsible for repairs and which policy responds depends on the lease and on the cause of the water. A tenant, a landlord and a business each carry different coverage. Ask each insurer before work is signed off. Our logs and photos can support what you tell them, but they do not decide coverage.

Downtown Keene water damage questions

Do you work on buildings with shops and apartments together?

Yes. These are common downtown, and water often crosses from one use to another. We map moisture in every affected unit and keep a separate record for each so owners and tenants can use it.

Who should I call first, you or the landlord?

Both. If water is actively running, call us at once. Then tell the owner or manager, because they control shutoffs and access to other units.

Can you work around my business hours?

We plan the job with you once we see the scope. Some drying equipment can run overnight, and what can be moved to off hours is discussed on site.

Is downtown in the Beaver Brook floodplain?

Parts of Keene near the brook are mapped as floodplain, but we cannot confirm status for a downtown address. Check FEMA's flood map or ask your insurer.

What records do I need for my insurer?

Photos, the date and cause of the water, a list of damaged items and our moisture readings if you hire us. Your insurer decides what is covered.
